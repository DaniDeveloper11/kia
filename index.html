What We Build

Structural Concrete & Shotcrete Services

From the first footing to the final finish coat, KIA crews handle the structural concrete and shotcrete scope general contractors can’t afford to get wrong — on jobs statewide.

Structural Concrete

Cast-in-place structural concrete for foundations, columns, shear walls, and elevated decks engineered to carry heavy structural and seismic loads.

  • Structural walls & columns
  • Elevated slabs & decks
  • Subterranean parking structures
  • Mix design per engineer specs
  • Third-party testing coordination

Shotcrete & Gunite (Wet & Dry Mix)

Certified wet-mix and dry-mix shotcrete crews for retaining walls, pool shells, and slope stabilization where conventional formwork isn’t practical.

  • Wet-mix & dry-mix application
  • Gunite application
  • Nozzlemen certified to ACI 506
  • Rebound control & finish work
  • Slope & vertical surface application
  • Reservoir, canal & marine linings

Foundations & Footings

Spread footings, mat slabs, and deep foundation systems built to engineered tolerances for commercial, industrial, and infrastructure loads.

  • Spread & continuous footings
  • Mat & raft foundations
  • Grade beams
  • Vapor barrier & rebar placement

Retaining Walls & Shoring

Earth retention systems — from soil nail walls to temporary shoring — engineered to protect adjacent structures and keep excavations safe.

  • Shotcrete-faced retaining walls
  • Temporary excavation shoring
  • Lagging & soldier pile systems
  • Hillside concrete construction

Concrete Repair & Restoration

Structural repair, crack injection, and spalling restoration that extend the service life of aging concrete assets without a full rebuild.

  • Spalling & delamination repair
  • Structural crack injection
  • Rebar corrosion remediation
  • Protective coatings & sealants

Seismic Retrofitting

Shear wall additions, shotcrete jacketing, and foundation strengthening engineered to bring structures up to current seismic code.

  • Shotcrete shear wall jacketing
  • Soft-story retrofits
  • Foundation & footing strengthening
  • Code-compliance coordination

Slabs

Precision-finished slabs-on-grade placed to flatness and levelness tolerances, from subgrade prep through the final finish.

  • Slab-on-grade placement & finishing
  • Subgrade & vapor barrier prep
  • Rebar & welded wire reinforcement
  • Control joints & sawcutting

Caissons & Deep Foundations

Drilled caissons and deep pier systems for sites where spread footings won’t carry the load — hillside builds, poor soils, and high-rise column loads.

  • Drilled caissons & piers
  • Friction & end-bearing shafts
  • Casing & slurry placement
  • Geotech & inspection coordination

Pools

Shotcrete pool and spa shells for residential, resort, and commercial aquatic projects — shot by the same ACI 506 nozzlemen who run our structural work.

  • Shotcrete pool & spa shells
  • Steel placement per engineer specs
  • Raised bond beams & vanishing edges
  • Pool decks & surrounding hardscape

How We Work

Our Process

A consistent, documented sequence on every statewide job — from first call to closeout.

01

Consultation

We review your drawings, timeline, and site conditions to scope the work and flag constructability issues early.

02

Engineering & Estimate

Our estimators build a detailed, itemized proposal aligned to the structural specs — mix design, volumes, and schedule included.

03

Planning & Permits

We coordinate submittals and mix designs with your GC and the engineer of record before crews mobilize.

04

Concrete / Shotcrete Placement

Certified crews execute the pour or shotcrete application to spec, with daily progress reporting back to your team.

05

Quality Control

In-house QC checkpoints and testing coordination confirm strength, cover, and finish before we call it done.

06

Delivery & Warranty

Final walkthrough, closeout documentation, and a workmanship warranty back every completed placement.
